DIRK v0.7.1 source-level audit fixes (UNVALIDATED HARDWARE BUILD)

- Bound UART RX work (768 bytes / 1.6 ms per update) to reduce frame starvation.
- Increase C5 serial line length from 220 to 384 chars and queue from 16 to 32.
- Detect overflowing C5 UART lines and queued record loss rather than passing truncated partial records.
- Limit C5 AP record parse work to 12 per main-loop iteration.
- Allow 5GHz records without an explicit ssid or scan text if BSSID, valid channel, and RSSI exist.
- Avoid duplicate wardrive live tick invalidation and priority-inversion from header telemetry.
- Retain FIX8 2.4GHz async scanner and synchronous fallback.
