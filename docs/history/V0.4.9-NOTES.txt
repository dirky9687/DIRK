DIRK v0.4.9

Visual / wardrive polish pass:
- Splash uses the menu DIRK mark as the single source of truth.
- Splash mark scales to 156x51 instead of crude 2x blocks.
- Blue mode recolors only red-dominant accent pixels; neutral logo detail stays neutral.
- Red splash replaces the long sword-like graphic with a short broad double-edged dirk, pronounced guard, wrapped grip and pommel.
- Wardrive companion redesigned as an ASCII-first tracked recon buddy.
- Buddy now patrols across the screen instead of wobbling in one fixed location.
- Left/right/fire/GPS-lost ASCII poses are selected dynamically.
- Speech bubble swaps sides based on buddy position and retains event/XP quips.
- World now has two parallax layers, scrolling terrain, road markers and moving landmarks.
- Wardrive live view refresh target tightened to 85 ms for visibly smoother travel.
- Existing v0.4.8 Wi-Fi signal tracker, BLE explorer/detail, wardrive backend, 2.4/optional C5 5 GHz/GPS/BLE logging preserved.
