DIRK v0.7.3 – Wardrive frame clipping + MonsterC5 evidence build

What changed
1) Menu animation retained without modifications from working v0.7.2.
2) Wardrive: dynamic scene now renders into the existing 240x117 sprite
   and transfers only the lower 240x82 region via the LGFX display clip
   rectangle (35..116) every 90 ms. This reduces transferred pixels by
   ~30% per animated frame, without allocating another sprite. The C5/GPS
   counters and status header are updated separately at most once/s or on
   a header state change. The XP strip remains in the dynamic region.
3) C5 diagnostics: 4 pages with RX/TX bytes/lines, drop/overlong counts,
   scan starts/completions, parser reject categories, last scan command,
   last raw AP candidate and last raw UART line. Left/right move pages;
   Tab starts a C5 scan. The raw record pages should help identify the
   REAL installed C5 firmware's output format and any UART issue.
4) Parser counts 2.4 GHz records separately; 5GHz classification unchanged.
   C5 UART line buffer increased to 768 characters to avoid dropping a
   longer but valid projectZero/JanOS reply.
5) Splash and wardrive logging version strings updated to 0.7.3.

IMPORTANT
- These are test/debug changes. No live Cardputer ADV + MonsterC5
  testing and no PlatformIO compile was possible in this environment.
- This does not flash/update JanOS on the separate MonsterC5 module.
- We cannot guarantee 5GHz discovery until the UART diagnostic output
  proves scan commands reach the C5 and the actual record format is known.
- If still no 5GHz SSIDs, take photos of all 4 pages after waiting 20-25
  seconds on the 5G Diagnostics screen. Also test a known 5GHz-only SSID
  fixed to a non-DFS channel such as 36 or 149 (where locally permitted).
- Independent C5 hardware test: confirm a known 5GHz SSID appears using
  the official C5Lab companion firmware (separate from DIRK) and confirm
  JanOS/projectZero is installed on the MonsterC5 board itself.
